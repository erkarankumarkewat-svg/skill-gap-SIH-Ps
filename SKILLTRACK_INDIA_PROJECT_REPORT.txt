1. EXECUTIVE OVERVIEW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SkillTrack India is an outcome intelligence and longitudinal tracking layer designed
specifically for the Indian skilling ecosystem. Existing portals (e.g., Skill India 
Digital, PMKVY portals) effectively record enrollment, batch attendance, and certificate
issuance. SkillTrack India answers the harder, longitudinal question:
"What happened to a trainee after training, and can we trust that information?"

The platform connects:
Training -> Certification -> Versioned Consent -> Multi-Channel Follow-up -> 
Outcome Ingestion -> Employer OTP Verification -> Wage Progression -> 
Livelihood Retention -> Skill Gap Intelligence -> District Policy Insights.

The implementation was built following strict phased deliverables:
- Phase 0: Research & Architecture
- Phase 1: Foundation (Backend, Database, Schema, RBAC, Auth)
- Phase 2: Outcome Engine (Intake, Verification, Provenance, Provenance State Machine)
- Phase 3: Analytics & Intelligence (Retention, Wage Trajectories, NLP Classifier)
- Phase 4: Demo Polish (750+ Scale Cohort, 5 Hero Cases, Single-Page UI, Connectors)

PHASE 1: FOUNDATION
- Initialized Node.js + Express + TypeScript + Prisma backend environment.
- Configured normalized relational database schema with 25+ relational models.
- JWT-based authentication system with salted bcrypt password hashing (10 rounds).
- Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) middleware enforcing permissions:
  * SYSTEM_ADMIN: Full system control and audit access
  * DISTRICT_ADMIN: District aggregated views & provider benchmarking
  * PROVIDER: Scoped strictly to own enrolled batches and trainees
  * TRAINEE: Scoped strictly to own outcome passport and consent records
  * EMPLOYER: Scoped to tokenized employment verification requests
- Persistent Trainee ID generation (STI-YYYY-XXXXXX), decoupling trainee identity 
  from volatile mobile numbers and SIM changes.
- Append-only immutable consent ledger tracking versioned purpose approvals.
- Deliverables generated:
  * backend/prisma/schema.prisma
  * backend/src/index.ts
  * backend/src/db.ts

PHASE 2: OUTCOME ENGINE
- Follow-up campaign scheduling engine with configurable cadences (3M, 6M, 9M, 12M).
- Mock WhatsApp Webhook receiver to ingest natural conversational responses.
- Longitudinal outcome reporting covering:
  * Formal Employment
  * Self-Employment / Micro-Enterprises (including income ranges)
  * Apprenticeships
  * Unemployed (Looking vs Not Looking)
- Time-series wage record ingestion (Monthly, Hourly, Stipend, Income Range).
- Employer Verification Portal with 6-digit cryptographic OTP token generation 
  (15-minute expiration, attempt throttling).
- Outcome Provenance Engine: Automates the transition from SELF_REPORTED to 
  EMPLOYER_VERIFIED or SYSTEM_DERIVED without overwriting historical self-reports.
- Employer feedback mechanism capturing observed skill deficiencies during verification.
- Deliverables generated:

PHASE 3: ANALYTICS & INTELLIGENCE
- Executive Dashboard analytics engine calculating:
  * Verified Employment Rate (%)
  * Self-Employment Rate (%)
  * Formal Employment Rate (%)
  * Apprenticeship Rate (%)
  * 3M, 6M, 9M, and 12M Livelihood Retention Curves
  * 5-Stage Outcome Provenance Funnel (Certified -> Contacted -> Responded -> Placed -> Verified)
  * Employment Mix Distribution
- Time-series wage progression analytics (Starting placed wage vs 6M vs 12M growth).
- Employer Skill Gap Aggregator: Compares employer deficiency reports against course skill tags.
- Small-Sample Differential Privacy Protection: Enforces strict k-anonymity (n >= 5) 
  suppressing metrics with "INSUFFICIENT SAMPLE FOR PRIVACY" to prevent deanonymization.
- Explainable NLP Attrition & Reason Classifier:
  * Parses unstructured natural language trainee feedback into standard taxonomy 
    (TRANSPORTATION_BARRIER, WAGE_EXPECTATION_GAP, FAMILY_RESPONSIBILITIES, etc.).
  * Returns deterministic confidence score, matched linguistic concepts, and audited explanation.
- Contactability Risk Score Engine: Evaluates bounce history, elapsed time, and alternate 
  contact availability to assign LOW, MEDIUM, or HIGH outreach risk.
- Deliverables generated:
  * backend/src/routes/analytics.ts
  * backend/test-phase3.ts (Automated Analytics & Privacy Test)
